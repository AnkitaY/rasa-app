## Voice table
| Attribute | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Tone | Warm, practical, grounded | Preachy, clinical, hustle-y |
| Person | "you" and "your" | "our users", "customers" |
| Benefit | Specific ("save 30 min Sunday") | Vague ("transform your life") |
| Health | Convenience, variety, organization | Weight loss, calories, restriction |
| Humor | Warm, light, occasional | Forced, try-hard |

## Never say
- "Revolutionary" / "game-changing" / "seamless" / "easy" (overused)
- Any language implying users are failing at health goals
- "Successfully" / "Error:" / "failed" — these are cold and clinical
- Competitor names in public content

## UI copy examples (from existing app)
| Context | Good (current app) | Bad |
|---|---|---|
| Error | "Hmm, something went wrong. Give it one more try?" | "Error: Request failed." |
| Success | "Your week is sorted. 5 dinners, zero decision fatigue." | "Meal plan generated successfully." |
| Onboarding prompt | "Anything we should never put on the menu?" | "Please select your dietary restrictions." |
| Empty state | "Your recipe bank fills up the moment you plan your first week." | "No recipes found." |

## Channel conventions
| Channel | Length | Format |
|---|---|---|
| App copy | Shortest possible | Verb-first action labels |
| Error messages | 1-2 sentences | What happened + what to do |
| Email subject | ≤50 chars | Curiosity or direct benefit |
| Blog post | 400-700 words | Practical how-to framing |
